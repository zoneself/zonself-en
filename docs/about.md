# About — GuXiao Automotive Rubber Parts Manufacturer

## Company Name

Hebei Xingtai GuXiao Rubber Products Co., Ltd.

## Introduction of this site

Xingtai GuXiao Rubber Products Co., Ltd. is a professional automotive rubber parts manufacturer , specializing in the production of high-quality rubber components for steering systems, suspension systems, drivetrain systems, engine sealing systems, and hydraulic applications .

Located in Tiankou Town, Renxian County, Xingtai City, Hebei Province, China, our factory focuses on precision manufacturing of critical rubber parts such as CV Joint Boots, Driveshaft Boots, Steering Rack Boots, Shock Absorber Boots, Stabilizer Bar Bushings, Rubber O-Rings, and Valve Cover Gaskets .

With in-depth experience in OEM & ODM custom rubber parts manufacturing , we provide both standard automotive rubber components and fully customized solutions based on drawings, samples, or application requirements.

Our products are widely used for oil leak prevention, dust protection, sealing performance enhancement, and NVH reduction , helping improve vehicle reliability and service life under harsh operating conditions.
